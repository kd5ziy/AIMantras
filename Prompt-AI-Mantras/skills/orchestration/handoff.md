# Handoff

## Purpose
Transfer work cleanly between personas with all necessary context, ensuring continuity and preventing information loss. This skill enables effective multi-persona collaboration.

## When to Use
- Transitioning from planning to execution
- Passing work from domain persona to evaluator
- Escalating issues to specialized personas
- Resuming work after interruption
- Documenting completion for the next phase

## Inputs Required
- **From**: Persona handing off
- **To**: Persona receiving
- **Work product**: What has been completed
- **Context**: Relevant background and decisions made
- **Next steps**: What the receiving persona should do
- **Constraints**: Boundaries and requirements to maintain
- **Success criteria**: How to know the work is done

## Outputs Produced
- Structured handoff document
- Work product summary
- Decision log
- Open questions or concerns
- Clear next actions
- Success criteria for receiving persona

## Invocation
```
[Applying handoff skill]
From: {source persona}
To: {target persona}
Work product: {what was completed}
Context: {relevant background}
Next steps: {what to do next}
Constraints: {boundaries to maintain}
Success criteria: {definition of done}
```

## Constraints
- Cannot transfer tacit knowledge automatically
- Receiving persona may need clarification
- Should not include unnecessary information
- Must preserve critical context
- Handoff overhead should be proportional to complexity
