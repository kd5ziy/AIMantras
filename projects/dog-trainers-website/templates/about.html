About Pawsitive Training

Dedicated to building stronger bonds between dogs and their families.

Our Story

Founded in 2015, Pawsitive Training began with a simple mission: to help dogs and their owners live happier lives together. What started as a one-person operation has grown into a team of passionate, certified trainers who share a commitment to humane, effective training methods.

We believe that every dog can learn, and every owner can become a confident leader. Our approach focuses on understanding canine behavior, building communication, and creating positive experiences that make training enjoyable for everyone involved.
